# Office Presence 2.1.0

Android build for the pilot. versionCode 16. Needs the matching server update.

## What changed

- New welcome steps after sign in: read the privacy notice, then allow location, notifications and battery. Tracking starts only after you agree. You > Privacy shows what is kept and lets you withdraw.
- Visits keep the points they were recorded with, even if an office's points change later.
- Mock location fixes are flagged and do not count for real accounts.
- A visit counts only from a trusted phone location at an office. A rejected fix does not change your points. Developer shows the last rejection.
- A weekly summary on Monday 9:00 am replaces the daily one. You also get a notice when a correction is approved or not approved, and when tracking stops working.
- Too many sign-in attempts show a countdown before you can try again.
- Month shows holidays and your leave as records only. They do not change your 8 point target. You can mark a working day with no visit as leave.
- A notice tells you when HR adds an exception, for example "Fri 13 Nov counts as 1 point (Gurugram)."
- Managers see You > My team, with how many people are below target this month. It opens the team page in your browser.

## Install

1. Download the signed release APK (GitHub Release, /download, or Firebase App Tester).
2. On Android, allow installs from your browser (Install unknown apps).
3. Uninstall any debug or staging build first if the signing key differs from this release.
4. Install, sign in with your PwC email, accept the privacy notice, and allow location all the time.
