# Office Presence privacy notice

Office Presence counts the days you work from a PwC office. This notice explains what the app records, who can see it, and how long we keep it.

## What we collect

- Location fixes taken when your phone is at or near a PwC office: the time, the office matched, and how accurate the fix was.
- The Wi-Fi network name seen inside an office, when your phone reports it.
- The name of the phone you signed in on (for example "Pixel 8").
- Your In and Out times at each office, and the visit days and points they add up to.

## What we do not collect

- Location outside offices is not stored. When a fix does not match an office, we keep only the time and that it was outside, not where you were.
- We do not track you on non-working use. Outside offices the app makes at most a single check to see if you have arrived.
- We do not read your contacts, messages, photos, or other apps.

## Why

- To count visit days and points toward the monthly office target.
- To show you your own month, and to let admins fix missing or wrong visits when you ask for a correction.

## Who can see it

- You: your visits, points, and hours.
- Admins: your visits, offices, times, and phone sync times. Admins never see coordinates.
- Colleagues: only if you turn on sharing. They see In or Out, since when, and the office name. Never hours, points, or location.
- Managers: your manager, and the lead of your team, see whether you are In or Out today and at which office, your month points against the 8-point target with HR exception days marked, your visit days for the month with office and first In and last Out times, and your correction requests and their status. This does not depend on colleague sharing. They never see coordinates, hours, device names, Wi-Fi, or the reason for an HR exception. Managers cannot edit visits or grant exceptions. If an admin allows it, your manager can approve or not approve your correction requests.

## How long we keep it

- Raw location (coordinates and Wi-Fi name) is cleared after {retentionDays} days.
- Visit totals (office, day, points) are kept for reporting.
- Sign-in codes are deleted after one day.

## Your choices

- Turn colleague sharing off at any time in You or Settings.
- Sign out of the app on any phone.
- Withdraw consent: tracking stops and you are signed out. Days you miss will not count unless an admin adds them. Your past visits are kept.

## Contact

Questions about this notice: contact the Office Presence admins or the PwC privacy team.
